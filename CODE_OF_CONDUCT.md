# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **Contribute Wing** a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate toward other contributors
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Being patient with beginners — this repository exists to help people learn
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately disruptive contributions
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer, **Muhammad Mustafa ([@Syntrojex](https://github.com/Syntrojex))**, by opening an issue or contacting directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
